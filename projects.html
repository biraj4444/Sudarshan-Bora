What the app does

Real-time rides

A ride request reaches drivers as it happens, and both sides see its status update without refreshing.

Live GPS map

The passenger follows the vehicle on a map while the driver navigates to the pickup and drop.

Payment integration

Fares are paid online inside the app, so there is no fumbling for change.

Budget-friendly driver choice

Passengers compare drivers and pick the deal that suits their budget.

Passenger site

Book a ride, choose a driver, track the trip and pay.

Driver site

Accept rides, follow the route and manage earnings.

Admin site

Oversee drivers, rides and payments from a single dashboard.

Built for e-vehicles

Designed around electric rickshaws and other e-vehicles for short, affordable trips.
